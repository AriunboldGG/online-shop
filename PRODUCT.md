# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Shoppers in Mongolia buying Korean products, browsing and buying mostly on their phones. They want to find items by season, feel confident the goods are genuinely Korean, and get through checkout quickly.

Open decision: whether the primary audience is young adults, families, or both has not been confirmed.

## Product Purpose

An online shop that sells products imported from Korea to customers in Mongolia. Success means a shopper can find a seasonal item on a phone, trust that it is authentic, understand the price, size and delivery, and complete checkout without friction.

## Positioning

Every product is imported from Korea, and the catalog is organized by season (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter) rather than by category alone. Authenticity of Korean origin is the core promise.

## Operating Context

- Mobile-first: most sessions happen on phones.
- Customers browse by season, then by product type.
- Purchases depend on clear delivery information for orders within Mongolia.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Product categories: furniture (also called "mebel" by customers), home goods, clothing, and shoes.
- Seasonal collections: Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter.
- Interface language: Mongolian.
- Currency: Mongolian tögrög (MNT, ₮).
- Must always show: clear prices, size guides for clothing and shoes, and delivery information.
- Checkout must be short and fast on mobile.
- Stack: Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4.
- Undecided: payment methods, delivery regions and fees, return policy, and the shop's name.

## Brand Commitments

- Shop name: "Online Shop".
- Logo and voice are still open. Never copy another brand's logo, mascots, or text.

## Evidence on Hand

No product photos, catalog data, proof of import or authenticity, customer reviews, or delivery terms exist in the project yet. Future work must not invent reviews, ratings, certifications, or delivery promises; use clearly marked placeholders until real content is supplied.

## Product Principles

1. Trust first: make Korean origin and authenticity visible and believable, never claimed without evidence.
2. Phone first: every flow must work comfortably one-handed on a small screen.
3. No surprises: price, size, and delivery are clear before checkout, not discovered during it.
4. Season is the way in: the seasonal collections are the primary path through the catalog.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable text sizes and large tap targets are required. Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA.
